Sport and diabetes: where to start?

Physical activity can be a helpful part of life for people with diabetes. The important thing is to choose an activity that feels comfortable and plan it safely.

Explore sports

Sports to try

Here are a few activities that many people enjoy. The best choice depends on the person.

🚶

Walking

Walking is simple and does not need special equipment. You can start with a short, easy walk.

🏊

Swimming

Swimming works many muscles and is gentle on the joints. Choose a pace that feels right for you.

🚴

Cycling

Cycling can be a fun way to stay active. Start on a safe route and take breaks when needed.

🧘

Yoga

Yoga includes gentle movements and breathing exercises. It can also help with relaxation.

Before exercising

  • Talk to your healthcare professional before starting a new activity.
  • Ask how and when to check your blood glucose around exercise.
  • Keep any recommended supplies and fast-acting carbohydrates nearby.
  • Stop if you feel unwell and follow your personal care plan.

Exercise can affect blood glucose in different ways, so advice should be individual.
